About the channel

The Golden Tales is an independent YouTube channel of original animated short videos. Each episode tells a true story about money in under a minute: the deals, decisions and twists behind famous fortunes, and the lesson inside each one. Every video is written, animated, narrated and edited by the channel's creator.

Golden Tales Uploader

Golden Tales Uploader is a private, single-user tool used only by its operator, the creator behind The Golden Tales. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the creator's own finished videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to YouTube channels the creator owns (currently The Golden Tales).
